Design and Implementation of Light Body Cloud Manager Based on WeChat Mini Program
Computer Science and Technology  Huayi Huang
Supervisor  Nanchao Luo

Abstract：Weight management has become a national strategy. The 2025 "National Weight Management Year" proposed the construction of a digital weight management system. However, China faces prominent problems of overweight and obesity, and the weight loss market is in disarray. Existing health management tools suffer from single functions, insufficient personalization, and low adaptability. To align with policy guidelines and meet domestic demands, this study develops the "Light Body Cloud Manager" WeChat Mini Program, a lightweight weight loss management tool. The system adopts a layered architecture: the user terminal integrates core modules such as health data recording, personalized plan generation, solar term weight loss topics, and community interaction; the professional coach terminal provides customized plans and online consulting services; the administrator terminal realizes the full-process management of professional qualification authentication, user management, community information, and feedback. Technically, MySQL is used for database construction, Python for back-end service development, and uni-app for the mobile front-end interface
System testing shows that the performance fully meets the design expectations.
Keywords：Weight Management; uni-app; Scientific Weight Loss; Solar Term Health Preservation
